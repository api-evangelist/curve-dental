At a Glance Manual billing workflows delay cash flow and increase accounts receivable every month Paper statements, phone calls, and portals introduce friction that causes patients to postpone payments Disconnected payment tools burn staff hours on reconciliation, follow-ups, and error correction Rising AR leads to higher write-offs and tighter operating margins over time Embedded text-to-pay systems reduce friction and accelerate patient collections immediately Modern payment infrastructure protects revenue, reduces workload, and stabilizes cash flow
