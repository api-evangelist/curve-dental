At a Glance Startup dental practices often have limited staff, making operational efficiency and workflow simplicity especially important. The practice management platform chosen during startup can influence patient experience, financial insight, team productivity, and long-term growth. Many startup owners evaluate software based on four key factors: cloud-native architecture, integrated workflows, ease of training, and scalability.
